Getting started

  1. Add an assignment. Choose Add assignment and enter its title, course and due date.
  2. Find your next deadline. My assignments lists unfinished work in deadline order. Use search or the course filter to narrow the list.
  3. Finish or correct a task. Open My assignments and filter by course, this week (Monday to Sunday), month or year. Use To-do notebook for personal notes. Select an assignment to write notes or set its progress. Tick its checkbox to move it to Completed. Use Edit to correct it, or uncheck it in Completed to reopen it.
